1. About this policy

Desi Duel: 2 Player Games ("Desi Duel", "the app", "we", "our" or "us") is a two-player party game for Android, published by Vashudev Games. This Privacy Policy explains what information the app handles, how it is used, and the choices you have.

2. What we do and do not collect

We do not require you to register, sign in, or provide your name, email address, phone number or precise location. We do not run a server that receives your gameplay data.

The app does not ask for access to your contacts, photos, camera, microphone or precise location.

The only information that leaves your device is the information handled by the third-party services described in section 4, mainly to show ads.

3. Information stored on your device

To make the game work, the app saves the following on your device only:

  • Player nicknames and avatar choices you enter
  • Match results, head-to-head records against other players, statistics and achievements
  • Coins, unlocked items, themes and daily challenge streaks
  • Your settings, such as sound, haptics and language

We do not collect this information or have access to it. If you choose to share a result card, the image is passed to the app you select in Android's share menu, and that app's own policy applies.

4. Third-party services

The app uses the following services. They may collect information as described in their own policies.

ServiceWhat it may processWhy
Google AdMob (Google Mobile Ads SDK) Advertising ID, IP address, device and app information, and ad interaction data To show and measure ads (interstitial and rewarded video) and limit how often ads appear
Google User Messaging Platform (UMP) Your ad consent choice, stored on your device To ask for and respect your consent where required
Firebase Crashlytics and Analytics Crash reports, device model, operating system version, app version and general app usage events To find and fix crashes and improve the games
Google Play (billing) Purchase details for the optional "Remove Ads" purchase, handled by Google To process purchases. We do not receive your payment card details.

Learn more:

Ads are shown only on the results screen after a match or when you choose to watch a rewarded video. They are never shown during a match or countdown.

5. Advertising ID and your choices

The app may use the Android advertising ID, a resettable identifier on your device, for advertising, measuring ads, and limiting how often you see the same ad. Where required, the app asks for your consent before showing personalized ads. If you do not consent, ads may be non-personalized.

You can:

  • Change your ad consent choice in the app under Settings, where available.
  • Reset or delete your advertising ID or opt out of ad personalization in your Android system settings (usually under Privacy or Google, then Ads).
  • Manage Google ad preferences at adssettings.google.com.
  • Buy "Remove Ads" in the app, if available, to stop interstitial ads.

6. Notifications

The app can send optional reminders, such as a notice about a new game. These are scheduled on your device and do not involve sending your data to us. You can turn them off in the app's Settings or in Android's notification settings at any time.

7. Children

Desi Duel is intended for people aged 13 and older. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child under 13 has used the app, please contact us at the address below and use your device settings to remove the app and its data.

8. Backups, retention and deletion

Your game data stays on your device until you delete it. Uninstalling the app or clearing its storage in Android settings removes it. The app's Settings may also include an option to reset your progress.

If Google backup is turned on for your device, Android may back up app data, including your progress and nicknames, to your Google account. This is handled by Google and your device settings, not by us.

9. Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights over personal data, such as access, correction, deletion, or withdrawing consent. We do not hold personal data about you on our own servers, so most of these controls are available directly on your device as described above. For data handled by Google services, use the links in section 4. You can also contact us with any request or question.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time, for example when we add features or services. Changes will be published on this page with an updated date at the top.

11. Contact us

If you have questions or requests about this Privacy Policy, contact Vashudev Games at support@vashudevgames.com.
